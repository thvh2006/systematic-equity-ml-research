What would change the decision?

A separately specified new study with credible execution and independent evaluation. The delayed-fill sensitivity is not permission to replace the primary rule after seeing its test.

Data acceptance and boundaries

The selected wrappers are not a survivorship-free ETF universe or constant historical sector membership. Raw provider records are excluded from the repository.

Feature availability

All features use signal-close information. Learned scaling and cuts use matured training rows. Source history can contain later corrections.

Development-only model selection

Development 2020–2022. Only its selected candidate enters the primary saved test; no post-test reselection. Momentum also beats the selected ML policy in development.

Predeclared cost and delay challenges

A further-session delay reverses ML versus momentum rankings. Treat this as implementation sensitivity. It is not a test-selected replacement strategy.

Independent first-rebalance calculator

Local calculation from nine review quotes, not model retraining or a complete backtest. It recalculates quantities, fees, cash and marked NAV.

What the sample can support

The circular block interval describes mean paired monthly differences in the observed sample. It is not a CAGR interval, PBO estimate, multiple-testing correction or forecast probability.

Known limits

  • Fixed selected funds and changing sector classifications.
  • Corrected historical feed without archived provider vintages; rounded prices.
  • Opening-mark sizing and daily-volume capacity proxy; no auction-spread calibration.
  • Zero cash return and no separate tax model.
  • 36 test months; dependent fund rows and limited regime observations.
  • A stored local freeze is not independently timestamped preregistration.
