Scope and limits

Source prices are raw, with events accounted separately. Ex-date dividends become receivables and cash arrives on pay date. Split shares roll before trading; delisting cash closes held positions. Reverse/fractional splits are rejected until a cash-in-lieu policy is implemented.

The fixture calendar uses weekdays and synthetic session times, not exchange holidays. Session-volume participation is not proof of opening-auction liquidity. Whole shares, no leverage, sells-before-buys and expired unfilled orders are explicit assumptions. No live order or network request is made by this file.
