## Strengths

- A dated information timeline and matured training labels constrain direct look-ahead; scaling and stump cuts are learned within the eligible training set.
- All strategies share a whole-share cash/action engine with costs. The ledger separates dividend entitlements from their later cash payment and avoids double counting slippage.
- The report distinguishes IC, absolute return, relative economic value and descriptive uncertainty. It does not select the favourable delayed execution result after seeing the test.
- The public verification path is clear about what can be checked without private records. Source-vintage, data licensing, selection and execution limits remain explicit.

## Remaining limitations and worthwhile next work

1. **Data confidence:** corrected/rounded retrospective history, fixed fund wrappers and unverified original volume units limit point-in-time and capacity claims. Stronger evidence requires appropriately permitted, archived provider vintages and independent action/unit reconciliation.
2. **Execution confidence:** sizing at the opening mark and daily-volume participation do not establish actual auction fills. Validate with historical auction/spread evidence before making implementability claims.
3. **Statistical confidence:** 36 monthly observations and six bounded candidates do not eliminate broader research-selection risk. The block interval includes zero and is not an equivalence test or a family-wide selection correction.
4. **Test coverage (addressed in release 0.4):** an additional future-price/volume perturbation test now checks the selected stump fit, scores, top-three choices and an eligible earlier portfolio fill. The original ridge test is retained. These finite cases do not establish the absence of every possible leakage channel.
5. **Operations:** no prospective signals or realised paper sessions exist. Fresh permitted data, timestamped forecasts and incident reconciliation are prerequisites for a forward study. Keep the stale-data block active.

These points bound the existing conclusion. They do not call for modifying the consumed holdout or searching until ML beats a baseline.
